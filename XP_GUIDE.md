# 🎮 SmartSpeak XP System Guide

## How to Earn XP

SmartSpeak rewards you with XP (Experience Points) for completing various learning activities. Here's how to earn XP:

### 📚 Vocabulary Learning (10 XP per word)
- **How**: Go to Learning → Vocabulary
- **Actions that earn XP**:
  - Complete flashcard reviews
  - Mark words as "learned"
  - Complete vocabulary quizzes
  
### 💬 Dialogue Practice (20 XP per dialogue)
- **How**: Go to Learning → Dialogues
- **Actions that earn XP**:
  - Complete a full dialogue
  - Record your voice for each turn
  - Reach the end of the conversation

### 📝 Grammar Exercises (Score × 10 XP)
- **How**: Go to Learning → Grammar
- **Actions that earn XP**:
  - Complete grammar tests
  - Your score (e.g., 8/10) × 10 = 80 XP
  
### 🎯 Mini-Games (Variable XP)
- **The Debater**: 50 XP for completing a debate
- **The Negotiator**: 100 XP for successful deals, 30 XP for attempts

### 📖 Module Completion (100 XP per module)
- **How**: Go to Classes → Grade 9
- **Actions that earn XP**:
  - Complete all vocabulary in a module
  - Pass the module test
  - Mark module as complete

## XP Levels

Your XP determines your "Level Number":
- **Level 1**: 0-99 XP
- **Level 2**: 100-199 XP  
- **Level 3**: 200-299 XP
- **Level 4**: 300-399 XP
- And so on...

**Formula**: Level = (Total XP ÷ 100) + 1

## Current Issue ⚠️

**Note**: The XP system currently has a bug where XP is not being saved to the database automatically. This means:
- XP shows in your sidebar during the session
- But resets when you refresh the page

**Workaround**: Your real proficiency level (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2) is saved correctly and won't reset.

## Checking Your XP

1. Look at the sidebar - it shows "Level X" and "XP: XXX"
2. Go to Profile → Your progress bar shows total XP
3. Activity calendar shows which days you were active

## Tips for Fast XP Gain

1. **Daily Vocabulary** (150 XP/day): Review 15 words
2. **Complete Dialogues** (60 XP): Do 3 dialogues
3. **Grammar Tests** (~ 80 XP): Take 1 grammar test
4. **Mini-Games** (50-100 XP): Play negotiator or debater

**Total possible per day**: ~400 XP = 4 levels!
